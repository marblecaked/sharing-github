## Service Intervals & Schedules

### BMW Condition Based Service (CBS) System

The 2017 M2 uses BMW's Condition Based Service (CBS) system, which monitors:
- Engine load
- Driving style
- Operating conditions
- Mileage and time

**CBS monitors and alerts for:**
- Engine oil
- Front and rear brake pads
- Brake fluid
- Vehicle inspection
- Various component conditions

### Standard Service Intervals

#### Oil Service
- **Interval:** Every 10,000 miles OR 12 months (whichever comes first)
- **Aggressive driving:** Can shorten to 6,000-8,000 miles
- **Track use:** Change oil after every 2-3 track days or 5,000 miles
- **Note:** CBS may extend to 15,000 miles for highway driving, but 10,000 is recommended maximum

#### Major Service Milestones

| Mileage | Service Items |
|---------|---------------|
| **10,000 miles** | • Oil & filter change<br>• Brake inspection<br>• Tire rotation<br>• Coolant level check<br>• Visual inspection |
| **20,000 miles** | • Oil & filter change<br>• Cabin air filter<br>• Tire rotation<br>• Brake fluid test |
| **30,000 miles** | • Oil & filter change<br>• Engine air filter<br>• Brake fluid flush<br>• Spark plugs (turbocharged)<br>• Tire rotation |
| **40,000 miles** | • Oil & filter change<br>• Differential fluid change<br>• Manual transmission fluid check<br>• Tire rotation |
| **50,000 miles** | • Oil & filter change<br>• Manual transmission fluid change<br>• Tire rotation |
| **60,000 miles** | • Oil & filter change<br>• Spark plugs (if not done at 30k)<br>• Engine air filter<br>• Cabin air filter<br>• Brake fluid flush<br>• Coolant system inspection<br>• Fuel filter<br>• Full vehicle inspection |
| **80,000 miles** | • Oil & filter change<br>• Differential fluid change<br>• Water pump inspection/replacement (preventive)<br>• Cooling system hoses inspection |
| **100,000 miles** | • All 60k services PLUS<br>• Thermostat inspection<br>• Suspension bushing inspection<br>• Control arm inspection<br>• Full brake system overhaul consideration |

## Engine Specifications & Service

### N55B30T0 Engine Details

**Engine Type:** Inline-6, Turbocharged, Direct Injection
**Valvetrain:** DOHC, 24 Valves, Variable Valve Timing (VANOS - Double VANOS)
**Turbocharger:** Single twin-scroll turbo (Borg Warner)
**Compression Ratio:** 10.2:1
**Fuel System:** Direct Injection (High Pressure Fuel Pump)
**Engine Management:** Bosch MEVD17.2.G ECU

### Oil Service

#### Oil Specifications
- **Viscosity:** 5W-30 (Recommended: BMW LL-01 Specification)
- **Capacity:** 6.9 quarts (6.5 liters) with filter
- **Recommended Oils:**
  - BMW TwinPower Turbo 5W-30
  - Castrol Edge Professional LL01 5W-30
  - Mobil 1 FS 0W-40 (European Car Formula)
  - Liqui Moly Leichtlauf High Tech LL 5W-30
  - Pennzoil Platinum Euro 5W-30
  - Shell Helix Ultra Professional 5W-30

#### Oil Filter
- **OEM Part Number:** 11427848321 (Standard)
- **Mann Filter:** HU 932/6 n
- **Mahle:** OX 404D

#### Oil Change Procedure
1. Warm engine to operating temperature
2. Lift vehicle securely on jack stands or lift
3. Remove oil drain plug (17mm socket)
   - **Torque spec:** 25 Nm (18 lb-ft)
   - Replace copper crush washer each time
4. Remove oil filter housing (36mm socket or specific tool)
5. Replace O-rings on filter housing
6. Install new filter
7. Reinstall filter housing
   - **Torque spec:** 25 Nm (18 lb-ft)
8. Fill with new oil (6.5L)
9. Run engine, check for leaks
10. **Reset CBS indicator** via iDrive or OBD tool

### Spark Plugs & Ignition Coils

#### Spark Plug Specifications
- **OEM Part Number:** 12 12 0 037 607
- **NGK Equivalent:** 97506 SILZKBR8D8S (Iridium)
- **Gap:** Pre-gapped, do not adjust
- **Quantity:** 6 plugs
- **Torque:** 23 Nm (17 lb-ft)
- **Tool Required:** 14mm thin-wall 12-point spark plug socket

#### Replacement Interval
- **Turbocharged N55:** Every 30,000-45,000 miles
- **Stock tune:** 45,000 miles acceptable
- **Modified/Tuned:** 30,000 miles recommended
- **Track use:** Check every 15,000 miles

#### Ignition Coil Specifications
- **OEM Part Number:** 12 13 8 616 153
- **Replacement:** As needed when failure occurs
- **Common Symptoms:** Misfire codes (P0301-P0306), rough idle, poor fuel economy

### Air Filters

#### Engine Air Filter
- **Part Number:** 13 71 7 571 355
- **Interval:** 30,000 miles (or sooner if dusty conditions)
- **Aftermarket Performance Options:**
  - K&N 33-2997
  - aFe Pro 5R
  - BMC Air Filter

#### Cabin Air Filter
- **Part Number:** 64 31 9 272 528 (Standard)
- **Activated Charcoal:** 64 31 9 313 519
- **Interval:** 15,000-20,000 miles
- **Location:** Behind glove box

### Valve Cover Gasket

**Common Issue:** Oil leaks from valve cover gasket are prevalent on N55 engines

#### Symptoms:
- Oil smell when engine is hot
- Oil residue on valve cover
- Oil dripping onto exhaust manifold
- Burning oil smell in cabin

#### Replacement Parts:
- Valve cover gasket kit: 11 12 7 570 292
- **Interval:** Replace at first sign of leaking (typically 60,000-100,000 miles)
- **DIY Cost:** $100-150 (parts)
- **Shop Cost:** $800-1,200

### Oil Filter Housing Gasket

**Common Issue:** Another frequent leak point on N55 engines

#### Symptoms:
- Oil seeping where housing meets cylinder head
- Oil on black coolant hose underneath
- Low oil warning without visible external leaks

#### Replacement Parts:
- Oil filter housing gasket kit: 11 42 8 637 821
- **Interval:** Replace at first sign of leaking (typically 60,000-100,000 miles)
- **DIY Cost:** $50-80 (parts)
- **Shop Cost:** $800-1,000

## Manual Transmission Service

### 6-Speed Manual Transmission (Getrag)

**Transmission Code:** GS6-45BZ
**Type:** 6-Speed Manual, Dog-leg 1st gear pattern
**Same transmission used in:** E82 1M, F80/F82 M3/M4, G87 M2

### Transmission Characteristics
- Stiff and rubbery shift feel (improves with break-in)
- High clutch engagement point
- Tall gear ratios (especially 1st gear)
- Improved through software tuning over generations
- **Torque Limit:** 406 lb-ft (M2 Competition at 406 lb-ft is at limit)

### Transmission Fluid Service

#### Fluid Specifications
- **Capacity:** ~1.7 liters (1.8 quarts)
- **OEM Fluid:** BMW MTF-LT-5 (83 22 2 282 671)
- **Alternatives:**
  - Redline MT-90 75W-90 GL-4
  - Motul Gear 300 75W-90
  - Royal Purple Synchromax
- **Viscosity:** 75W-90 or equivalent

#### Service Interval
- **BMW Official:** "Lifetime fill" (~100,000 miles)
- **Recommended:** 50,000-60,000 miles
- **Track use:** Every 30,000 miles or annually
- **Spirited driving:** 40,000-50,000 miles

#### Fluid Change Procedure
1. Lift vehicle securely
2. Remove fill plug first (to ensure you can refill)
   - 17mm hex socket
3. Remove drain plug
   - 17mm hex socket
4. Drain completely
5. Reinstall drain plug with new washer
   - **Torque:** 60 Nm (44 lb-ft)
6. Fill through fill hole using fluid pump
   - Fill until fluid begins to seep out
7. Reinstall fill plug with new washer
   - **Torque:** 60 Nm (44 lb-ft)

### Clutch System

#### Clutch Specifications
- **Type:** Single dry clutch
- **Diameter:** 240mm
- **Hydraulic actuation**

#### Clutch Fluid
- **Type:** DOT 4 brake fluid (shares reservoir with brakes)
- **Service:** Replace with brake fluid service every 2 years

#### Clutch Lifespan
- **Street driving:** 60,000-100,000+ miles
- **Spirited/Track:** 30,000-50,000 miles
- **Modified high torque:** 20,000-40,000 miles

#### Symptoms of Wear:
- Slipping under acceleration
- High engagement point
- Difficulty shifting
- Burning smell
- Chatter/vibration

## Differential Service

### M Variable Limited-Slip Differential

**Type:** Electronically controlled multi-plate limited-slip
**Differential Ratio:** 3.46:1

### Differential Fluid Service

#### Fluid Specifications
- **Capacity:** ~1.0 liter
- **OEM Fluid:** BMW SAF-XO (83 22 0 440 214)
  - 75W-140 synthetic gear oil
  - Specifically formulated for M Variable LSD
- **Alternatives (use with caution):**
  - Redline 75W-140 Shockproof Gear Oil
  - Motul Gear 300 LS 75W-90

#### Service Interval
- **BMW Official:** "Lifetime fill"
- **Recommended:** 40,000-50,000 miles for street driving
- **Track use:** Every 20,000-30,000 miles or every other oil change
- **Spirited driving:** Every 40,000 miles

#### Common Issue: Differential Seal Leaks
- **Symptom:** Oil leaking from differential seals around 15,000+ miles
- **Most common on:** M2 Competition, but can affect standard M2
- **Solution:** Replace seals under warranty if applicable

#### Fluid Change Procedure
1. Lift rear of vehicle
2. Remove fill plug first
   - 14mm hex socket
3. Remove drain plug
   - 14mm hex socket
4. Drain completely
5. Reinstall drain plug with new washer
   - **Torque:** 45 Nm (33 lb-ft)
6. Fill through fill hole using fluid pump
   - Fill until fluid reaches bottom of fill hole
7. Reinstall fill plug with new washer
   - **Torque:** 45 Nm (33 lb-ft)

  - Front: 28mm
  - Rear: 22mm

#### Aftermarket Options
- Brembo OE Replacement
- Zimmermann Sport
- StopTech Slotted
- Girodisc 2-piece (performance upgrade)

### Brake Fluid Service

#### Fluid Specifications
- **Type:** DOT 4 Low Viscosity
- **OEM Fluid:** BMW Brake Fluid DOT 4 LV (83 13 2 405 977)
- **Alternatives:**
  - ATE TYP 200 DOT 4
  - Motul RBF 600 (performance/track)
  - Castrol SRF (track use)

#### Service Interval
- **Standard:** Every 2 years or 30,000 miles
- **BMW Recommendation:** Initial at 30,000, then every 60,000
- **Recommended for M cars:** Every 2 years regardless of mileage
- **Track use:** Before each track season (every 12 months minimum)

#### Fluid Capacity
- Total system: ~1.0 liter

#### Bleeding Procedure
1. Use proper brake bleeding equipment or two-person method
2. Bleeding sequence: RR → LR → RF → LF
3. Keep reservoir topped up during bleeding
4. Bleed until fluid runs clear at each caliper
5. Check for firm pedal feel
6. Top off reservoir to MAX line

## Cooling System Service

### Cooling System Overview

The F87 M2 has an extremely complex cooling system with **5-6 different radiators and heat exchangers** across **3-4 separate cooling circuits**.

#### Main Components:
- Main radiator
- Auxiliary radiators (multiple)
- Engine oil cooler
- Transmission cooler
- Turbo coolant circuit
- Electric water pump
- Expansion tank
- Thermostat
- Multiple hoses and connections

### Coolant Specifications

#### Coolant Type
- **OEM Coolant:** BMW Coolant/Antifreeze (Blue) - 83 19 2 211 191
- **Mixture:** 50/50 with distilled water
- **Color:** Blue
- **Type:** Ethylene glycol-based, phosphate-free
- **Capacity:** ~7.5 liters (total system)

#### Service Interval
- **BMW Recommendation:** Initial fill is "lifetime"
- **Realistic interval:** 60,000-80,000 miles or 4-6 years
- **Track use:** Inspect annually, replace every 3-4 years

### Water Pump

**Type:** Electric water pump (common failure point on BMW)

#### Lifespan & Replacement
- **Typical failure:** 60,000-80,000 miles
- **Recommended preventive replacement:** 60,000 miles
- **Critical:** Replace before failure to avoid being stranded

#### Symptoms of Failure:
- Overheating
- Coolant warning lights
- Loss of heat from cabin
- Engine temperature fluctuations
- Unusual noises from front of engine

#### Replacement Parts:
- Water pump: 11 51 7 588 885
- **DIY Cost:** $200-300 (parts)
- **Shop Cost:** $800-1,200

### Expansion Tank

**Common Issue:** Plastic expansion tanks can crack over time

#### Symptoms:
- Coolant loss
- Visible cracks in tank
- Coolant smell
- Low coolant warnings

#### Replacement:
- Part number: 17 13 7 609 469
- **Interval:** Replace at first sign of cracking
- **DIY Cost:** $50-100
- **Shop Cost:** $200-400

### Thermostat

**Type:** Electronically controlled map thermostat

#### Replacement Interval:
- **Preventive:** 80,000-100,000 miles
- **As needed:** At first sign of failure

#### Symptoms of Failure:
- Overheating
- Slow to reach operating temperature
- Temperature fluctuations
- Check engine light (thermostat codes)

#### Replacement Parts:
- Thermostat: 11 53 7 549 476
- **DIY Cost:** $100-150
- **Shop Cost:** $400-700

### Cooling System Hoses

**Caution:** Factory hoses are a known weak point

#### Replacement:
- **Inspection:** Every 60,000 miles
- **Preventive replacement:** 80,000-100,000 miles
- Replace if any signs of:
  - Cracking
  - Bulging
  - Softness
  - Leaking

## Fuel System

### Fuel System Overview

**Type:** Direct Injection
**Fuel:** Premium Unleaded (91+ octane, 93 octane recommended)
**Tank Capacity:** 13.5 gallons (51 liters)

### High Pressure Fuel Pump (HPFP)

#### Specifications
- **Type:** Mechanical, engine-driven
- **Pressure:** 100-200+ bar (1,450-2,900+ psi)
- **Function:** Pressurizes fuel from low-pressure pump for direct injection

#### Stock HPFP Limitations
- **Stock capacity:** Sufficient for stock tune
- **Stage 1 tune:** HPFP is at maximum capacity
- **Higher power:** Upgrade required

#### Common HPFP Failure Symptoms:
- Long crank/hard starting (especially cold)
- Loss of power
- Hesitation, stumbling, jerking
- Engine misfires
- Fuel pressure codes

#### HPFP Upgrade Options (for tuned cars):
- **B58TU HPFP** (from Mini JCW F56 / Toyota Supra)
  - Direct replacement
  - ~20-25% more flow
  - Part number: 13 51 7 616 170
- **TTFS HPFP Upgrade**
  - Plug-and-play
  - Increased flow for tuned applications
- **Nostrum S55 HPFP**
  - 36% more flow than stock
  - For high horsepower builds

### Fuel Injectors

#### Stock Injectors
- **Type:** Direct injection
- **OEM Part Number:** 13 53 7 637 929 (per injector, 6 required)

#### Replacement Interval:
- **As needed** when failure occurs
- **Typical lifespan:** 100,000+ miles

#### Symptoms of Failure:
- Rough idle
- Misfires
- Poor fuel economy
- Fuel smell
- Check engine light (injector codes)

### Fuel Filter

**Location:** In fuel tank (part of fuel pump module)

#### Replacement Interval:
- **BMW recommendation:** 60,000 miles
- **Realistic:** Rarely needs replacement if using quality fuel
- **If experiencing fuel delivery issues:** May require replacement

## Electrical System

### Battery

#### Battery Specifications
- **Type:** AGM (Absorbed Glass Mat)
- **Group Size:** Group 49 (or H8)
- **CCA (Cold Cranking Amps):** 900+
- **Ah (Amp Hours):** 90-92 Ah
- **Voltage:** 12V
- **Location:** Engine bay, passenger side

#### OEM Battery
- BMW AGM Battery: 61 21 7 604 822 (or equivalent)

#### Aftermarket Options:
- Bosch S5 AGM
- Interstate MTX-H8
- Optima YellowTop D51R (Group 51R - may fit)

#### Battery Lifespan:
- **Typical:** 4-6 years
- **Factors:** Climate, driving habits, electrical load

#### Battery Registration

**Critical:** BMW requires battery registration when replacing

**Why registration is necessary:**
- Power management system tracks battery age and condition
- Charging profile adjusts based on battery type and age
- Failure to register can cause premature battery failure

**How to Register:**
1. Use BMW diagnostic tool (INPA, ISTA, Carly, BimmerCode)
2. Select battery type (AGM vs Lead-Acid)
3. Input battery capacity (Ah)
4. Confirm registration

**Important:** If switching between AGM and Lead-Acid, you must CODE the change, not just register

### Alternator

**Output:** 180A (typical)

#### Lifespan:
- **Typical:** 100,000-150,000+ miles
- **Symptoms of failure:**
  - Battery warning light
  - Dimming lights
  - Electrical issues
  - Battery not charging

## Suspension & Steering

### Front Suspension

**Type:** MacPherson strut with aluminum lower control arms

#### Key Components:
- Struts/shocks
- Springs (M adaptive suspension)
- Control arms
- Ball joints
- Bushings
- Sway bar (anti-roll bar)
- End links

### Rear Suspension

**Type:** Multi-link with 5 control arms per side

#### Key Components:
- Shocks (M adaptive suspension)
- Springs
- Upper control arms
- Lower control arms
- Thrust arms
- Toe links
- Camber arms
- Bushings
- Sway bar
- End links

### Common Wear Items

#### Control Arm Bushings

**Lifespan:** 60,000-100,000 miles (varies by driving style)

**Symptoms of worn bushings:**
- Clunking noise over bumps
- Vague steering (especially at highway speeds)
- Irregular tire wear
- Popping noises over bumps
- Vehicle pulling to one side during hard braking
- Squeaking over bumps

**Most common failure point:** Lower rear outer control arm (swing arm) ball joint

**Replacement interval:**
- **Inspection:** Annually
- **Replacement:** Every 3-4 years or at first sign of wear

#### Ball Joints

**Inspection:** Annually
**Replacement:** Every 3-4 years or when play detected

#### Sway Bar End Links

**Lifespan:** 50,000-80,000 miles

**Symptoms:**
- Clunking from front or rear over bumps
- Rattling noise

### Wheel Alignment

**Recommended interval:**
- Every 10,000-15,000 miles
- After any suspension work
- If uneven tire wear noticed
- After hitting curbs or potholes

**Track use:** Before and after each track event

## Owner Tips & Best Practices

### Break-In Period (New/Used)

**First 1,200 miles:**
- Vary engine speed, avoid constant RPM
- No full-throttle acceleration
- Keep RPM below 4,500
- Avoid hard braking
- No track use

### Daily Driving Tips

1. **Warm up properly:**
   - Let engine idle 30 seconds before driving
   - Drive gently until oil temp reaches 180°F+
   - Full performance only when fully warm

2. **Cool down:**
   - After spirited driving, drive gently for 2-3 minutes before shutting off
   - Allows turbo to cool with oil circulation

3. **Premium fuel only:**
   - Use 91+ octane (93 recommended)
   - Avoid ethanol-heavy fuel when possible
   - Top-tier detergent gasoline recommended

4. **Check fluids regularly:**
   - Engine oil every 2-3 fill-ups
   - Coolant monthly
   - Brake fluid color annually

### Track Day Preparation

**Before track day:**
- Fresh brake fluid (within 6 months)
- Check brake pad thickness (minimum 5mm)
- Fresh engine oil (if due within 2,000 miles)
- Tire pressure check (adjust for track use)
- Inspect for leaks
- Check lug bolt torque

**At the track:**
- Check tire pressures after each session (hot)
- Inspect brake pads for cracks/excessive wear
- Check for fluid leaks
- Monitor oil and coolant temps

**After track day:**
- Inspect brake pads and rotors
- Check for leaks
- Oil change if multiple track days
- Alignment check if curbs were hit

### Storage (Winter/Long-term)

**Preparing for storage (30+ days):**
1. Change oil if due within storage period
2. Fill fuel tank, add stabilizer
3. Clean car thoroughly
4. Battery tender/maintainer
5. Increase tire pressure to 40 PSI
6. Use car cover (breathable)

**Starting after storage:**
1. Check tire pressures
2. Check all fluid levels
3. Start engine, let idle until warm
4. Test drive gently, check for issues

## Common Owner Questions

**Q: Can I use 0W-40 instead of 5W-30?**
A: Yes, Mobil 1 FS 0W-40 European Car Formula is widely used and BMW-approved. Some owners prefer it for track use.

**Q: How often should I really change the oil?**
A: 10,000 miles max for street, 5,000 miles for track/spirited use. Oil analysis can help determine optimal interval for your driving.

**Q: Is the manual transmission fluid really lifetime?**
A: No. While BMW says "lifetime," change at 50,000-60,000 miles for longevity. Track cars should change at 30,000.

**Q: What causes the cold start rattle?**
A: VANOS rattle on cold start is normal for N55. If excessive, check VANOS solenoids.

**Q: Why does my car burn oil?**
A: N55 engines can burn 1 quart per 1,000-2,000 miles, especially when driven hard. Check PCV valve and valve cover gasket. Excessive consumption may require engine work.

**Q: Should I use run-flat tires?**
A: M2 does not come with run-flats. Standard performance tires recommended for best ride and performance.

## Appendix: Warning Lights & Codes

### Common Warning Lights

**Yellow Oil Can:** Low oil level - Check oil ASAP
**Red Thermometer:** Coolant temperature high - Stop safely, check coolant
**Yellow Brake Symbol:** Brake pad wear - Inspect pads soon
**Red Brake Symbol:** Brake system fault or fluid low - Check immediately
**Check Engine Light:** Emissions/engine fault - Scan for codes
**Yellow Battery:** Charging system issue - Check battery/alternator

### Common Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs)

**P0300:** Random/multiple cylinder misfire
**P0301-P0306:** Cylinder-specific misfire
**P0171/P0174:** System too lean
**P0420:** Catalyst efficiency below threshold
**P1545:** Throttle position not learned
**P163F:** Fuel pressure sensor
**P1082/P1083:** Valvetronic fault
