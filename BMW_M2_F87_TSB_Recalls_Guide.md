## Understanding TSBs and Recalls

### What is a TSB (Technical Service Bulletin)?

A Technical Service Bulletin (also called SIB - Service Information Bulletin by BMW) is:
- An official communication from BMW to dealerships
- Describes known issues and recommended solutions
- NOT a recall - repairs typically not free unless under warranty
- Provides updated repair procedures or parts
- May address customer complaints or improve reliability

### What is a Safety Recall?

A recall is:
- Issued when a safety defect is identified
- **Repairs are FREE** regardless of warranty status
- Required by NHTSA (National Highway Traffic Safety Administration)
- Mandatory to fix
- Dealership will repair at no cost to owner

### Difference Between TSB and Recall

| Aspect | TSB | Recall |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Cost** | Owner pays (unless warranty) | FREE |
| **Mandatory** | No | Yes (for safety) |
| **Safety Issue** | Usually no | Yes |
| **Who Performs** | Any mechanic | Dealer required |
| **Notification** | Not sent to owners | Mailed to registered owners |

## Safety Recalls

### Confirmed Recalls for 2016-2018 BMW M2 (F87)

#### Recall: Rear Sub-Frame Bolts

**Recall Number:** Not specified in available data (possibly related to prior M3/M4 recall)
**Date Issued:** November 3, 2016 (for M3/M4, M2 may be included)

**Vehicles Affected:**
- 2016 BMW M2 Coupe (confirmed)
- Possibly 2017 M2 (check by VIN)
- Also affected: 2015-2017 M3 Sedan, 2015-2016 M4

**Issue:**
During servicing of the rear differential for a prior service action, the rear sub-frame bolts may have been reused instead of being replaced, which may cause the bolts to loosen over time.

**Safety Risk:**
Loose rear sub-frame bolts may adversely affect vehicle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash.

**Remedy:**
BMW dealers will replace the rear sub-frame bolts with new bolts, free of charge.

**Status:** Contact dealer to verify if your specific VIN is affected

---

#### Recall: Fuel Injector Missing Damping Elements

**Recall Number:** R/2020/352 (UK recall number)
**Date Issued:** July 12, 2020

**Issue:**
Some injectors may be missing damping elements, which could impact fuel lines and cause fuel leaks.

**Safety Risk:**
Fuel leaks increase the risk of fire.

**Vehicles Affected:**
Specific production dates - check with dealer

**Remedy:**
BMW dealers will inspect and replace affected fuel injectors free of charge.

### Engine & Drivetrain TSBs

#### TSB: Turbocharger Wastegate Rattle

**Symptoms:**
- Rattling noise from engine bay at idle
- More noticeable when cold
- Turbo wastegate actuator rattle

**Solution:**
- Updated wastegate actuator
- Software update
- Some cases: Turbocharger replacement

**Status:** Common issue, may be covered under warranty if under 48 months/50,000 miles

---

#### TSB: Oil Consumption

**Issue:**
N55 engines may consume excessive oil (more than 1 quart per 1,000 miles)

**Causes:**
- Low-tension oil control rings (design characteristic)
- Faulty PCV valve
- Worn valve stem seals
- Piston ring issues

**Solution:**
- PCV valve replacement (Part: 11 15 7 531 423)
- In severe cases: Piston ring replacement or engine overhaul
- BMW requires oil consumption test before authorizing warranty repair

**Warranty Notes:**
- Must document consumption pattern
- Typically requires 1,000-mile test intervals
- Some owners report warranty coverage, others denied

---

#### TSB: Valvetronic System Faults

**Symptoms:**
- Check engine light
- Rough idle
- Poor performance
- Codes: P1082, P1083, P1085, P1087

**Causes:**
- Eccentric shaft sensor failure
- Valvetronic motor failure
- Mechanical binding

**Solution:**
- Replace eccentric shaft sensor: 11 37 7 548 387
- Replace Valvetronic motor if needed
- Software update

---

#### TSB: High Pressure Fuel Pump (HPFP) Failure

**Symptoms:**
- Long cranking time (especially cold starts)
- Loss of power
- Rough running
- Codes: P1082, fuel pressure related codes

**Solution:**
- HPFP replacement
- Check for fuel contamination
- Inspect cam lobe for wear

**Part Number:** 13 51 7 592 553 (OEM replacement)

---

### Cooling System TSBs

#### TSB: Water Pump Failure

**Issue:**
Electric water pumps are known to fail prematurely on BMW engines

**Symptoms:**
- Overheating
- Loss of cabin heat
- Coolant warning lights
- Temperature fluctuations

**Solution:**
- Replace water pump
- **Preventive replacement recommended at 60,000 miles**

**Part Number:** 11 51 7 588 885

**Warranty:** Some extended warranty coverage reported for early failures

---

#### TSB: Coolant Expansion Tank Cracking

**Issue:**
Plastic expansion tank may develop cracks

**Symptoms:**
- Coolant loss
- Visible cracks
- Coolant smell
- Low coolant warning

**Solution:**
- Replace expansion tank
- Inspect associated hoses

**Part Number:** 17 13 7 609 469

---

### Transmission & Drivetrain TSBs

#### TSB: Manual Transmission Shift Quality

**Issue:**
Stiff, notchy shifting (especially when cold)
High clutch engagement point

**Solution:**
- Transmission software update
- Clutch delay valve removal (modification, not TSB)
- Ensure correct MTF fluid

**Note:** Some shift stiffness is characteristic of this transmission, improves with break-in

---

#### TSB: Differential Seal Leaks

**Issue:**
Differential seals may leak oil, particularly on M2 Competition but can affect standard M2

**Symptoms:**
- Oil visible around differential
- Oil on undercarriage
- Low differential fluid

**Solution:**
- Replace differential output shaft seals
- Typically occurs around 15,000+ miles

**Warranty Coverage:** Often covered if under factory warranty

---

### Brake System TSBs

#### TSB: Brake Squeal/Noise

**Issue:**
Brake squeal, especially low-speed braking

**Causes:**
- Pad compound
- Rotor surface
- Environmental factors

**Solutions:**
- Brake pad replacement with updated compound
- Rotor resurfacing or replacement
- Anti-squeal shims
- Proper bedding-in procedure

**Note:** Some brake noise is normal for high-performance brake systems

---

### Electrical System TSBs

#### TSB: Battery Discharge/Drain

**Issue:**
Battery drains when parked

**Causes:**
- Modules not going to sleep
- Parasitic draw
- Battery age/condition

**Solution:**
- Software updates for various modules
- Battery replacement and proper registration
- Check for aftermarket accessories causing drain

## When to Request TSB Repairs

### Scenarios Where TSBs Apply:

1. **Experiencing described symptoms**
   - Match your symptoms to TSB description
   - Bring TSB to dealer as reference

2. **Preventive maintenance**
   - Some TSBs recommend preventive replacement
   - May need to pay if out of warranty

3. **During other service**
   - Request TSB check during routine service
   - Bundle repairs to save labor costs

4. **Before warranty expires**
   - Address all TSB items before 50,000 miles
   - Document everything

### What to Bring to Dealer:

- VIN
- TSB number (if known)
- Detailed description of symptoms
- When problem occurs
- How often problem occurs
- Any error messages or warning lights

## Warranty Coverage

### BMW New Vehicle Limited Warranty

**Coverage Period:** 4 years / 50,000 miles (whichever first)

**What's Covered:**
- Defects in materials or workmanship
- Most TSB repairs if within warranty period
- All recall repairs (no time/mileage limit)

**What's NOT Covered:**
- Normal wear items (brake pads, tires, wiper blades)
- Maintenance (oil changes, fluids)
- Damage from abuse, modification, or lack of maintenance

### BMW Powertrain Warranty

**Coverage:** 4 years / 50,000 miles

**Covers:**
- Engine
- Transmission
- Differential
- Drive shafts

### BMW Corrosion Warranty

**Coverage:** 12 years / unlimited miles

**Covers:**
- Rust-through perforation of body panels

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I have to go to the dealer for TSB repairs?**
A: For warranty or recall repairs, yes. For out-of-warranty TSB repairs, independent shops can often perform the work at lower cost.

**Q: How do I know if a TSB applies to my car?**
A: TSBs list applicable VIN ranges and production dates. Dealer can verify if your specific VIN is affected.

**Q: Will dealer perform TSB repair for free?**
A: Only if:
- Vehicle is under warranty AND
- TSB issue is a defect (not normal wear) AND
- Problem is present/documented

**Q: Should I request all TSBs be performed?**
A: Only if you're experiencing the specific symptoms. Many TSBs address rare issues or apply to specific build dates.

**Q: Can I get TSB repairs at independent shop?**
A: Yes, but:
- They need TSB documentation
- May not have BMW-specific tools
- Won't be covered by BMW warranty

**Q: What if dealer denies warranty coverage for TSB issue?**
A: Escalate to:
1. Service manager
2. BMW Customer Relations
3. BMW North America
4. Legal consultation if significant cost

**Q: Are there secret warranties?**
A: BMW occasionally offers "goodwill" coverage for common issues outside warranty. Ask dealer about goodwill repair programs.

## Appendix: TSB Number Reference

### How to Read BMW TSB Numbers:

**Format:** SIB XX XX XX

**Example:** SIB 12 05 24

- **First number (12):** System category
  - 11 = Engine
  - 12 = Fuel System
  - 13 = Exhaust
  - 23 = Transmission
  - 31 = Rear Axle/Differential
  - 34 = Brakes
  - 61 = Electrical

- **Second number (05):** Sub-category
- **Third number (24):** Specific bulletin number

### Common BMW TSB Prefixes:

- **SIB:** Service Information Bulletin
- **SI:** Service Information
- **TU:** Technical Update
- **RA:** Repair Advice
